# The Genesis: Deep Research

*Phase 1*

This is the **most important** part of the whole process. You will create the **"Master Document"** (the "Project Bible") — a technical report of 15 to 25 pages that details every component of your application.

## What the deep research prompt must contain:

### 1. Total Intent

A detailed explanation of the end goal. What does the software do? For whom? What problem does it solve? What value does it deliver?

### 2. Exploring Possibilities

A direct order for the AI to explore ALL the most effective technologies, languages and approaches on the market today.

### 3. Selecting APIs and Libraries

A request to identify the best APIs for each function, ensuring modern and stable technology.

### 4. Workflow Deduction

An instruction for the AI to fill in the gaps in the workflow that you may not have seen.

> **Estimated time:** Deep research (via Gemini Deep Research or Claude) takes a few minutes and browses many sources (documentation, repositories, technical articles) to synthesize the plan. Google describes multi-page reports produced in minutes, drawing on hundreds of sites.

## Download format: Markdown > PDF

Always download as `.md` (Markdown) or `.txt`. Markdown preserves the structure of headings, lists and code blocks in "raw" form, which makes it easier for AI agents to read and to bring into IDEs such as VS Code.
